---
name: Gala content alignment
description: Rules for keeping Gala copy, monetary claims, deadlines, disclosures, and registration paths consistent.
---

Public Gala copy must be kept in lockstep with the approved prospectus and current outreach materials, particularly monetary claims, sponsor deadlines, and tax disclosures. Do not publish a symposium registration CTA until the dedicated event page or final registration destination is ready.

**Why:** Conflicting sponsorship amounts or lapsed deadlines undermine donor confidence, and placeholder registration links create a broken attendee journey.

**How to apply:** Before changing Gala prices, dates, disclosure wording, or event CTAs, reconcile them against the current approved collateral. Use a clear “details coming soon” status rather than a speculative registration link when the destination is unavailable.
