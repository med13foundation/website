name: Website copy
description: Organizer-supplied editorial conventions for public MED13 website copy.
---

Do not use em dashes in public website copy.

**Why:** The organizer explicitly requested removing all em dashes from the website.

**How to apply:** Use commas, colons, parentheses, or sentence breaks as appropriate in both languages and directly rendered component text. Preserve meaning and leave ordinary hyphens and time-range en dashes unchanged.
