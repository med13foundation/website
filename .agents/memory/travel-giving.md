---
name: Travel giving
description: How to present family travel support when transfer accounts and restricted donation paths are not verified.
---

Keep the family-travel giving path separate from ordinary cash donations. Miles, points, and travel-designated gifts should direct supporters to a person who can confirm current program rules and the right destination; a general donation should be labeled as general.

**Why:** The supplied travel one-pager intentionally leaves transfer-account fields blank, and the existing donation flow has not been verified as earmarked for family travel. Publishing guessed instructions or implying restricted use would mislead supporters.

**How to apply:** When updating travel fundraising copy or CTAs, confirm transfer eligibility, account ownership, and any designated-fund process with the foundation before publishing instructions. Do not expose account numbers or assume tax treatment for loyalty points.
