---
name: Contrast on tinted surfaces
description: Brand-color contrast decisions for pastel backgrounds and hero gradients
---

Keep the existing tinted backgrounds and hero gradient colors. Use darker text on those surfaces when the requested brand teal and rust do not meet WCAG AA contrast; reserve the lighter shades for decorative fills, borders, and gradients.

**Why:** The user chose to retain the backgrounds rather than turn them white. The requested darker brand colors clear 4.5:1 on white but can fall below it on the site's off-white or pastel surfaces.

**How to apply:** For future brand-color changes, check the actual text/background pair rather than assuming a color that passes on white will pass on every tint. Preserve hero gradient appearance and layout while adjusting its text when necessary.
