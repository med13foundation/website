---
name: Privacy-aligned external resources
description: Organizer requirements for font delivery and embedded YouTube videos.
---

Keep site fonts self-hosted and use YouTube's privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com domain for embedded videos.

**Why:** The organizer explicitly requested these changes to align the site's resource loading with its privacy policy. Do not reintroduce Google Fonts requests or standard YouTube embeds during future visual updates.

**How to apply:** Preserve local font delivery when changing typography and use privacy-enhanced embeds when adding videos. This does not mean that YouTube makes no third-party requests or that the entire site has been audited for privacy compliance.
