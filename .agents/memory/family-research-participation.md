name: Family research participation
description: Distinguish the MED13 census connection form from study enrollment and biosample donation.
---

Distinguish census connection from research participation, but do not describe the natural history study and biorepository as independent options. Biorepository collection requires natural history study registration and consent before samples are collected. The census destination is the MED13 Community Connection Form, which gathers contact details and areas of interest. Submitting it is not research enrollment or research consent. Natural history participation starts with requesting Matrix access and following the study team's consent process. Biorepository donation means biological samples, not money. Direct families to the MED13 Foundation research team to schedule a collection, rather than sending them to coordinate directly with the partner.

**Why:** The organizer clarified that biorepository collection requires natural history study registration and consent. Earlier wording incorrectly described all three opportunities as independent. The census form collects community connection interests rather than study consent, so contacting the foundation does not itself enroll someone in research. The organizer also specified that collection scheduling go through the foundation's research contact.

**How to apply:** Keep census and enrolled-study counts distinct, label destinations clearly, and do not promise automatic enrollment, eligibility or sample collection arrangements.

Families registering for COMBINEDBrain Matrix must select **MED13** as their partner patient advocacy group (PAG).

**Why:** The organizer explicitly specified this registration instruction so families affiliate with the correct partner.

**How to apply:** Include the MED13 partner PAG selection whenever explaining Matrix registration, in both English and Spanish.
