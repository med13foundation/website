About MED13 syndrome
MED13 syndrome is a rare genetic condition first described in 2018. It mainly affects the developing brain: how a child learns, moves and communicates. Features vary from child to child and can include developmental delay, speech and language delays, intellectual disability, autism, ADHD, epilepsy, feeding difficulties, and heart or vision differences. There is no treatment for MED13 syndrome today.

About the MED13 Foundation
Families directly affected by MED13 syndrome established the MED13 Foundation. The Foundation runs a natural history study and biorepository and is developing MED13 stem cell lines with NeuCyte. The Foundation is building together with OPUS 132 (https://opus132.org) so that research infrastructure built for MED13 can reach people with other rare diseases.
