## 1. Save-the-date email (Bay Area community list)

**From:** Amy Krystosik <amy@med13.org> (not the Gmail address)
**To:** yourself. **Bcc:** the community list. Never Cc; some recipients have not disclosed their family's situation to anyone.
**Subject:** Save the date, Sept 27: "Hope Is a Practice," a Sunday morning with the MED13 community in Palo Alto

Dear friends,

I would love for you to join us on **Sunday, September 27 at 10:30 a.m.** at the Unitarian Universalist Church of Palo Alto for a service called **"Hope Is a Practice."** UUCPA is hosting it, and we are shaping it together with members of the rare disease and disability community.

The idea is simple. For families like ours, hope is not a feeling that arrives. It is something we practice, one ordinary day at a time, and something no one should have to practice alone. The morning will include readings and music, a short reflection from me, a "hope stars" activity that children and adults do side by side, and coffee hour afterward, where our MED13 team will be around for anyone who wants to talk.

A few things worth knowing up front:

- **Everyone is welcome,** of any faith or none. You do not need to be part of the congregation.
- **This is not a fundraiser.** There is no MED13 ask; the morning offering is the church's own.
- **Children are welcome.** They join for the story at the start and then head to the church's programs.
- **It is livestreamed and recorded,** as every UUCPA service is, so people who cannot be there can still join. If you would rather stay off camera, you can take part from your seat.

**Where:** UUCPA, 505 E Charleston Rd, Palo Alto, CA 94306 (free parking on site)
**Online:** [livestream link, to confirm with UUCPA]
**Add to calendar:** attached

No RSVP needed. Just come. If you have a question or would like to help shape the morning behind the scenes, reply to this note.

With gratitude,
Amy

Amy Krystosik
Co-founder and President, MED13 Foundation
a fiscally sponsored project of Rare Village Foundation

## 4. Before you hit send: the checklist

1. **Rev. Cat's sign-off on public wording.** It is UUCPA's service, livestream, and sanctuary. Send her section 1 and section 3 together and ask for a yes on both, plus the exact livestream URL and whether she wants the service listed on UUCPA's own calendar the same way.
2. **Livestream link.** Placeholder in both the email and the .ics. Do not send with a placeholder.
3. **Parking and accessibility line.** Confirm the lot, step-free entry, and whether hearing assistance is available. Rare disease families will read for this.
4. **Blair's arc is not mentioned anywhere in public copy.** Keep it that way until she has said how she wants credit handled.
5. **No family names, no diagnoses, no "featured family."** The only named person is you.
6. **Photo policy for the day.** Decide now whether MED13 will photograph the constellation board and coffee hour, and get the church's OK. Anything with a child's face needs a guardian-signed release before it appears on med13.org or in gala collateral.
7. **Sender address.** amy@med13.org, Bcc only. If the list is over roughly 100 names, send from your email platform (Mailchimp or similar) so it does not land in spam and so unsubscribes are honored.
8. **Log it.** This is a cultivation touchpoint, not a fundraising event. Tag attendees in the donor pipeline as "warm, Sept 27" so the gala follow-up two weeks later is personal.
