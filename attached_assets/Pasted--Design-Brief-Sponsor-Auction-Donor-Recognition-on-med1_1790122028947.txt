# Design Brief: Sponsor + Auction Donor Recognition on med13.org
**For: website designer | From: Amy Krystosik | Date: Sept 22, 2026 | Needed live: Sept 25**
**Companion policy: Sponsor_Recognition_Policy_Spec.md (board-approved rules; this brief implements them)**

## What we are building (3 pieces)

1. A "Thank You to Our Sponsors" section on med13.org/gala
2. A new "Community Champions" page (quarterly rotating recognition)
3. An "Auction & In-Kind Donors" list on the gala page, below sponsors

Nothing on the homepage. Homepage placement is reserved for $25,000+ sponsors and none exist yet. Please do not add sponsor logos anywhere except the placements in this brief.

## Piece 1: Gala page sponsor section

Placement: on med13.org/gala, directly below the ticket/registration call to action and above the auction preview. It should be visible without being the hero.

Section heading: **Thank You to Our Sponsors**
Subheading: *Their support underwrites this evening, so every gift made in the room goes directly to research.*

Two rows:

**Row 1, $2,500 tier, logos at 220px width, alphabetical:**
- Baylor Genetics, caption "Catalyst Sponsor", link to baylorgenetics.com
- NeuCyte, caption "Gala Film Sponsor", link to neucyte.com

**Row 2, smaller tiers:**
- EditCo at 165px, caption "Gala Supporter", link to editco.bio
- Pequeños Talkers at 120px, caption "Community Sponsor", link to their site

Rules that matter:
- Captions under logos, small caps or subdued style. The caption IS the tier; do not add stars, rankings, or "gold/silver" language.
- Logos link out with rel="sponsored" and open in a new tab.
- Alt text: "[Company name], [tier name] of the MED13 Foundation gala."
- No sponsor taglines, product descriptions, or slogans on the site. Name, logo, tier caption, link. (IRS acknowledgment rules; longer sponsor blurbs run in the printed program only.)
- Logo hygiene: never stretch, recolor, or crop. Baylor and NeuCyte files have white backgrounds; place all four on a white/neutral section background so they sit evenly. If the page section is dark, ask Amy for reversed files rather than boxing them.
- Build the row grid to take a fifth logo without redesign. Late sponsors may land this week.

## Piece 2: Community Champions page

New page at med13.org/champions (or /supporters), linked from the gala page footer line: "See all of our community supporters."

Purpose: rotating recognition for corporate sponsors and business donors under $25,000. Entries stay up for 3 months from gift date (rounded up to quarter end), then move to an archive list at the bottom of the same page under "Past Champions."

Layout: simple card or list, one line each: name (linked), tier or "In-Kind Donor", month recognized. No logos required at launch; names are fine. Design it so adding an entry is a 2-minute content edit, not a design task; Iza will maintain it.

Seed content at launch (Q4 2026, expires Dec 31): Baylor Genetics, NeuCyte, EditCo, Pequeños Talkers, plus the auction donor list from Piece 3.
