Amy Krystosik, PhD, MPH Co-founder and President
Amy is an epidemiologist and data scientist who became a rare disease founder after her son Arturo was diagnosed with MED13 syndrome. Determined to build the research infrastructure that didn't exist for their family, she co-founded the MED13 Foundation in 2024 to connect families, accelerate science, and create a path to treatment for a condition affecting approximately 200 individuals worldwide.
Amy brings a decade of experience building the kind of research programs rare disease families need. Before founding the MED13 Foundation, she spent six years as a data scientist at the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative, where she built products to accelerate rare disease research and contributed to the largest paired RNA and whole-genome sequencing dataset for predicting the effects of rare variants on gene expression. Earlier in her career, she spent ten years building global health research cohorts across multiple countries.
Amy earned a PhD in Epidemiology from Kent State University and an MPH in International Health and Development from Tulane University, and completed postdoctoral training at Stanford University School of Medicine. She is also co-founding Artana Bio, a precision medicine company using AI to accelerate rare disease therapeutic development.
